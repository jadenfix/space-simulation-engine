# Periodic spectral Poisson and Gauss-law audit

## Purpose

A periodic electrostatic PIC solver can report a small generic residual while
still mixing incompatible definitions of charge, potential, electric field,
zero modes, and Nyquist modes. Spacewind therefore includes an independent
Fourier-space audit under:

```text
assurance/include/spacewind/spectral_poisson.h
assurance/src/spectral_poisson.c
assurance/tests/test_spectral_poisson.c
assurance/tests/test_spectral_poisson_integration.c
```

The audit evaluates the same physical identities in three independent ways:

1. potential against deposited charge;
2. electric field against the negative potential gradient;
3. electric-field divergence against deposited charge.

It also compares field energy in sample and spectral representations and checks
the electrostatic `rho * phi` energy identity.

## 1. Transform convention

For `N` real samples on a periodic interval of length `L`, the forward transform
is

\[
\hat f_k
=
\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
f_j\exp\left(-2\pi i\frac{kj}{N}\right),
\]

with inverse

\[
f_j
=
\frac{1}{N}
\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}
\hat f_k\exp\left(2\pi i\frac{kj}{N}\right).
\]

The signed wave number is

\[
\kappa_k
=
\frac{2\pi}{L}
\begin{cases}
k, & k\le N/2,\\
k-N, & k>N/2.
\end{cases}
\]

The implementation uses direct, compensated `O(N^2)` transforms. This is
intentional: the assurance path favors transparent indexing and deterministic
small-grid checks over FFT performance or shared implementation details with
the production solver.

## 2. Poisson identity

For each nonzero mode,

\[
-\frac{d^2\phi}{dx^2}
=
\frac{\rho}{\epsilon},
\]

so

\[
R_{\phi,k}
=
\epsilon\kappa_k^2\hat\phi_k-
\hat\rho_k.
\]

The audit reports a physical-space-equivalent RMS residual using Parseval
normalization and the largest spectral coefficient divided by `N`. Both an
absolute charge-density tolerance and a relative operator tolerance are
required by the contract.

## 3. Electric-gradient identity

The electrostatic field must satisfy

\[
\mathbf E=-\nabla\phi.
\]

In one periodic dimension,

\[
R_{E,k}
=
\hat E_k+i\kappa_k\hat\phi_k.
\]

This detects a solver that obtains the correct potential but reconstructs the
electric field with a different sign, wave number, normalization, or grid
staggering.

## 4. Spectral Gauss law

The field must also satisfy

\[
R_{G,k}
=
\epsilon i\kappa_k\hat E_k-
\hat\rho_k.
\]

Poisson closure alone is insufficient. A separate Gauss residual ensures the
field actually consumed by the particle pusher remains compatible with the
charge source.

## 5. Zero-mode contract

Periodic Poisson inversion does not determine the `k=0` mode. A nonzero mean
charge has no periodic electrostatic solution without an explicit compensating
background or boundary/source model.

The audit therefore reports

\[
\bar\rho
=
\frac{\hat\rho_0}{N}
\]

and requires it to remain below an absolute or relative neutrality tolerance.
The root PIC model includes a declared neutralizing background; the integration
test checks the combined particle-plus-background charge.

A uniform electric field is likewise a harmonic component not generated by the
periodic scalar potential. Unless separately declared, the audit requires the
mean electric field to remain below its configured tolerance.

A constant potential is a gauge choice and is deliberately not rejected.

## 6. Even-grid Nyquist boundary

For even `N`, the mode `k=N/2` is its own conjugate and samples as

\[
(-1)^j.
\]

On a real collocated grid, its derivative is not representable as another real
sampled mode: the continuous spectral derivative is imaginary at the grid
points while `sin(pi j)=0`.

This is not hidden as numerical noise. The audit separately reports:

- Nyquist charge RMS;
- fraction of nonzero charge spectral power in the Nyquist mode;
- Nyquist electric-gradient residual;
- Nyquist Gauss residual.

Poisson potential can still close for a pure Nyquist charge while the collocated
electric field vanishes. Such a state fails the Nyquist and electrostatic-energy
gates and cannot be promoted.

The correct response is refinement, filtering with an explicit conservation
ledger, or a discretization that gives the relevant derivative a resolved
staggered representation—not silently loosening the tolerance after seeing the
result.

## 7. Energy identities

### Parseval representation

For the unnormalized forward transform,

\[
\sum_j E_j^2
=
\frac{1}{N}
\sum_k|\hat E_k|^2.
\]

The audit compares

\[
U_E
=
\frac12\epsilon\Delta x\sum_j E_j^2
\]

against the independently transformed spectral representation.

### Electrostatic energy

For a neutral periodic Poisson solution,

\[
U_E
=
\frac12\Delta x\sum_j\rho_j\phi_j.
\]

A mismatch can expose an unresolved Nyquist mode, incompatible field
reconstruction, sign error, transform normalization error, or nonconverged
operator.

These energies are reported per unit transverse area.

## 8. Manufactured and adversarial checks

The standalone deterministic suite includes:

- a two-mode analytic charge/potential/electric-field superposition;
- odd and even sample counts;
- six orders of magnitude of amplitude scaling in both directions;
- constant-potential gauge invariance;
- electric-field corruption;
- potential corruption;
- charge corruption;
- nonneutral charge;
- undeclared uniform electric field;
- a pure Nyquist mode that closes potential but fails field and energy gates;
- invalid-domain, invalid-tolerance, and nonfinite-input rejection.

The root-engine integration suite additionally:

- calls the actual `sw_pic1d_solve_poisson_spectral` implementation;
- verifies manufactured low modes through all three operator identities;
- corrupts the root electric field and requires rejection;
- adds a nonzero charge mean and confirms the skipped zero mode is exposed;
- exercises the root Nyquist behavior explicitly;
- audits an actual bounded particle-in-cell state after repeated PIC steps.

The deterministic receipts are:

```text
assurance/output/spectral_poisson_receipt.json
assurance/output/root_spectral_poisson_receipt.json
```

## 9. Promotion semantics

The overall audit passes only when all of the following pass:

- charge neutrality;
- absence of an undeclared uniform electric field;
- Nyquist charge-power bound;
- spectral Poisson identity;
- negative-gradient identity;
- spectral Gauss identity;
- Parseval energy identity;
- electrostatic `rho * phi` energy identity;
- finite values throughout.

Absolute and relative tolerances are emitted in the receipt. The audit never
uses an unresolved mode as evidence merely because another identity still
closes.

## 10. Remaining work

This audit verifies a periodic electrostatic operator. A propulsion-relevant
kinetic solver still requires:

- local multidimensional charge-conserving current deposition;
- Ampere-Maxwell field advancement;
- magnetic-divergence control;
- particle-plus-field Poynting closure per accepted timestep;
- field-plus-particle-plus-spacecraft momentum closure;
- Maxwell-stress and particle-momentum agreement;
- refinement in grid, timestep, particle count, domain size, and shape order;
- chamber-calibrated boundary and source conditions.

## Scientific boundary

Passing the spectral audit establishes a bounded discrete equation and
bookkeeping result. It does not establish that the plasma model is complete,
that a chamber reproduces the solar wind, that a transverse plasma-wing force
exists, or that any propulsion system is realizable.
